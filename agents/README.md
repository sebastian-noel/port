# Sebastian Noel portfolio plan

Planning started November 7, 2026. The plan includes monthly wins-only leaderboards, a standardized passage length (working interpretation of 30 words), a live 100 WPM ghost cursor, confirmed homepage features, coral as the current accent, and a firm $0 hosting/services budget. Supabase Free is the selected backend. The Next.js scaffold and CI are in place.

The goal is a personal, playful portfolio with enough clarity for someone to quickly understand Sebastian's work and enough depth to explore how individual projects were built. The defining elements are a cornflower-blue canvas, a typed-name introduction with a flipping tile wave, photography that reveals color, and a typing challenge against Sebastian's 100 WPM benchmark.
